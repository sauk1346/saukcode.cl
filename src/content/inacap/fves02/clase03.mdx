# Past Simple (Affirmative/Negative/Question)

Covertir a: 
- pasado simple afirmativo
- pasado simple negativo
- pregunta cerrada (yes/no)
- pregunta abierta 

:::box[1. Mario jugó tenis]
- Afirmativo: :reveal[Mario played tennis]
- Negativo: :reveal[Mario did not play tennis]
- Pregunta cerrada: :reveal[Did Mario play tennis?]
- Pregunta abierta: :reveal[Why did Mario play tennis?]
:::

:::box[2. Mi padre trabajó ayer]
- Afirmativo: :reveal[My dad worked yesterday]
- Negativo: :reveal[My dad did not work yesterday]
- Pregunta cerrada: :reveal[Did my dad work yesterday?]
- Pregunta abierta: :reveal[Where did my dad work yesterday?]
:::

:::box[3. Fuimos a la playa]
- Afirmativo: :reveal[We went to the beach]
- Negativo: :reveal[We did not go to the beach]
- Pregunta cerrada: :reveal[Did we go to the beach?]
- Pregunta abierta: :reveal[When did we go to the beach?]
:::

:::box[4. Yo y mi hermano caminamos ayer]
- Afirmativo: :reveal[Me and my brother walked yesterday]
- Negativo: :reveal[Me and my brother did not walk yesterday]
- Pregunta cerrada: :reveal[Did me and my brother walk yesterday?]
- Pregunta abierta: :reveal[Why did me and my brother walk yesterday?]
:::
