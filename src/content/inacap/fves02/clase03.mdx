# Past Simple (Affirmative/Negative/Question)

Covertir a: 
- pasado simple afirmativo
- pasado simple negativo
- pregunta cerrada (yes/no)
- pregunta abierta 

<Box title="1. Mario jugó tenis">
- Afirmativo: <Reveal>Mario played tennis</Reveal>
- Negativo: <Reveal>Mario did not play tennis</Reveal>
- Pregunta cerrada: <Reveal>Did Mario play tennis?</Reveal>
- Pregunta abierta: <Reveal>Why did Mario play tennis?</Reveal>
</Box>

<Box title="2. Mi padre trabajó ayer">
- Afirmativo: <Reveal>My dad worked yesterday</Reveal>
- Negativo: <Reveal>My dad did not work yesterday</Reveal>
- Pregunta cerrada: <Reveal>Did my dad work yesterday?</Reveal>
- Pregunta abierta: <Reveal>Where did my dad work yesterday?</Reveal>
</Box>

<Box title="3. Fuimos a la playa">
- Afirmativo: <Reveal>We went to the beach</Reveal>
- Negativo: <Reveal>We did not go to the beach</Reveal>
- Pregunta cerrada: <Reveal>Did we go to the beach?</Reveal>
- Pregunta abierta: <Reveal>When did we go to the beach?</Reveal>
</Box>

<Box title="4. Yo y mi hermano caminamos ayer">

- Negativo: <Reveal>Me and my brother did not walk yesterday</Reveal>
- Pregunta cerrada: <Reveal>Did me and my brother walk yesterday?</Reveal>
